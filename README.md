# 🧠 CheckIT
### 🚀 Your Company's Memory, Always Up to Date

## 📌 About the Project

CheckIT is an AI-powered knowledge base designed to help companies centralize, manage, and maintain their internal knowledge. It automatically builds a structured knowledge base from company documents and keeps it updated using information from Outlook emails and Microsoft Teams conversations.

Our main goal is to ensure that valuable information is never lost, even when experienced employees leave the company.

## ❗ The Problem

Companies often depend on the knowledge and experience of individual employees. When a senior employee leaves, they may take important information with them, and there is often not enough time to transfer all their knowledge to other team members.

Some of the main challenges are:

- 📂 **Outdated documentation:** Information shared through emails and Teams conversations does not automatically reach the company's knowledge base.
- 🕒 **Lost history:** Teams lose track of when, why, and by whom certain changes were made.
- 👥 **Knowledge dependency:** Important information remains in employees' minds instead of being accessible in a shared space.
- ✍️ **Time-consuming processes:** Employees have to spend valuable time documenting information manually.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📄 1. Document Upload
Upload documents in various formats, including PDF, Word, Excel, and CSV. The AI automatically extracts relevant information, versions, dates, and changes.

### 📧 2. Outlook & Microsoft Teams Integration
CheckIT analyzes emails and Teams conversations to identify relevant changes and automatically update the knowledge base. Any update can be reverted if necessary.

### 📜 3. Complete Change History
Every modification is recorded, including its date, type, and original source, ensuring transparency and traceability.

### 🔎 4. Semantic Search
Find information based on its meaning, even when different employees use different terms to describe the same thing.

### 🤖 5. Ask AI
Ask questions and receive practical answers based on company documents, messages, and previous changes. Every answer includes cited sources.

### 🔔 6. Renewal Reminders
Receive monthly reminders when documents need to be reviewed or renewed.

## 💡 Why CheckIT?

Unlike traditional knowledge management platforms such as Confluence, Notion, and SharePoint, CheckIT focuses on automatically maintaining company knowledge.

Its main advantages are:

- ⚡ Automatic updates from Outlook and Microsoft Teams.
- 📝 Less manual documentation.
- 📚 Complete and traceable change history.
- 🔍 Search that understands different terminology.
- 🤖 AI-generated answers with cited sources.
- 🎯 Minimal disruption to employees' daily activities.

CheckIT works in the background, allowing teams to focus on their priorities instead of spending time maintaining documentation.

## 🔗 CheckIT and MCP

While MCP (Model Context Protocol) allows AI systems to connect to external tools and data sources, CheckIT goes a step further by focusing on how company knowledge is organized, updated, and preserved. MCP provides a standardized way to access information, but it does not inherently maintain a structured knowledge base or track changes over time. CheckIT addresses these needs by automatically identifying updates from Outlook and Microsoft Teams, preserving a complete history of modifications, and providing AI-powered answers with cited sources. Rather than simply connecting AI to existing information, CheckIT aims to transform scattered company data into a reliable, continuously updated knowledge base.
